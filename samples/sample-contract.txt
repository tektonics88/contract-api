MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the
Effective Date by and between Acme Vendor LLC ("Provider") and the
undersigned customer ("Client").

1. SERVICES
Provider shall provide the software development and consulting services
described in one or more Statements of Work ("SOW").

2. TERM AND AUTO-RENEWAL
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an
initial term of one (1) year. Thereafter, this Agreement shall
automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms unless Client
provides written notice of non-renewal no later than ninety (90) days
prior to the end of the then-current term. Fees may increase by up to
fifteen percent (15%) upon each renewal at Provider's sole discretion.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
Client shall pay all invoices within fifteen (15) days of receipt. Any
amount not paid when due shall accrue interest at the rate of one and
one-half percent (1.5%) per month. Provider may suspend all services
immediately if any invoice is more than five (5) days past due.

4. TERMINATION
Provider may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or no
reason, upon ten (10) days written notice to Client. Client may terminate
only for Provider's uncured material breach following a sixty (60) day
cure period. Upon termination, all prepaid fees are non-refundable.

5. INDEMNIFICATION
Client shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Provider and its
officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all
claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including
attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to this Agreement, regardless
of the cause and even if caused by Provider's own negligence.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
IN NO EVENT SHALL PROVIDER'S TOTAL LIABILITY EXCEED ONE HUNDRED DOLLARS
($100). PROVIDER SHALL NOT BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, OR
CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES. CLIENT'S LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT
BE SUBJECT TO ANY CAP OR LIMITATION.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work product, deliverables, inventions, and materials created by
Client in connection with this Agreement, including any pre-existing
Client materials incorporated therein, shall be the sole and exclusive
property of Provider.

8. NON-COMPETE
During the term and for a period of three (3) years thereafter, Client
shall not, anywhere in the world, engage or invest in any business that
competes with Provider or engage any of Provider's personnel.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY
Client shall keep confidential all information disclosed by Provider. This
obligation survives indefinitely. Provider has no corresponding
confidentiality obligation with respect to Client's information.

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered in
Provider's home jurisdiction. Client waives any right to a jury trial and
any right to participate in a class action. The prevailing party shall be
Provider.

11. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which
Provider maintains its principal place of business, without regard to
conflict-of-laws principles.

12. ASSIGNMENT
Provider may assign this Agreement to any party without notice or consent.
Client may not assign this Agreement without Provider's prior written
consent, which may be withheld for any reason.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the
Effective Date.
